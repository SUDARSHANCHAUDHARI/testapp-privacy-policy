# TestApp Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 2026
_Last updated: April 2026_
**Version:** 1.0.0

TestApp ("we," "our," or "us") is a test application developed by SudarshanTechLabs. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Information We Collect

**TestApp does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- No account or registration required.
- No analytics or crash reporting.
- No advertising SDKs.

### Data Stored Locally
- Any test data or preferences entered in the app are stored locally on your device only.

## How We Use Your Information

All functionality runs locally on your device. No data is sent to any server.

## Storage and Retention

- All data is stored exclusively on your device.
- Data is cleared when you uninstall the app.

## Data Security

- Local data is protected by Android's built-in storage encryption.

## Permissions Used

Only the minimum permissions required for the specific test functionality are requested.

## Your Rights and Controls

- Clear app data via Android Settings → Apps → TestApp → Clear Data.
- Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

## Children's Privacy

TestApp is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
